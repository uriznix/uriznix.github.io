### Story Worth Reading
**Trump Unveils New Tariffs on 14 Trading Partners**

Why: This is the administration's main attempt to rebuild its tariff leverage after the Supreme Court struck down its emergency-law duties in February, this time on the court-tested footing of Section 301. The design matters as much as the headline rates: maximum levies of 20% to 40% that would be capped at negotiated deal levels, a China rate tied to a truce expiring on 10 January, exemptions for some technology and Section 232 goods, and a timeline that pushes any duties into early 2027. Markets first sold off and then rallied as investors decided the plan was softer than feared, while Beijing, Brussels, Tokyo and others pushed back on the overcapacity premise.
