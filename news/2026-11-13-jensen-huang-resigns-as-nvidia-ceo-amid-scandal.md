## Morning Brief: Jensen Huang Resigns as Nvidia CEO Amid Scandal

### World

- Iranian Revolutionary Guards claim they destroyed a US early-warning radar in Bahrain in retaliation for a US strike on telecom infrastructure in Sirik, marking the first such claimed attack on Gulf-based US assets since September amid ongoing regional tensions.
- US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff report progress after talks with Putin in Moscow and Zelenskyy in Ireland, including agreement on the largest prisoner exchange of the Ukraine war and European participation in the next negotiation round.
- Israeli strike on Nasser Hospital in Gaza kills at least six, including a healthcare worker, as the conflict continues.

### Business

- Nvidia shares closed down 4.3% Thursday after Jensen Huang’s resignation (following an early plunge of nearly 12%), contributing to broader AI sector pressure and wiping significant market value.
- Microsoft faces a prolonged global outage affecting Outlook, Teams, Copilot and other 365 services for over 12 hours across 74 countries, disrupting businesses, governments and schools; company reports no cyberattack and partial recovery efforts.
- IEA projects the global oil market returning to surplus as Gulf exports recover, even as Middle East supply risks persist.

### Tech

- Jensen Huang resigns immediately as Nvidia CEO, president and board member after admitting an affair with an alleged Chinese intelligence asset and reports of a relationship with a subordinate; CFO Colette Kress named interim CEO while board searches for permanent successor.
- Microsoft outage stems from server infrastructure failures (not a traffic surge or attack), with recovery setbacks as restored servers fail under load; impacts millions of users worldwide.
- FBI seizes $1.2 billion in cryptocurrency linked to a $6.3 billion Binance heist attributed to North Korean actors.

### Overnight Market Moves

US equity futures are mixed to slightly lower ahead of inflation data, with S&P 500 futures near flat to -0.2%, Nasdaq 100 down about 0.2-0.4%, and Dow little changed to modestly positive; Microsoft shares pressured in premarket, Nvidia volatility continues, 10-year Treasury yield around 5.13-5.14% amid rate-hike bets, and oil mixed on Middle East risks versus surplus forecasts.
